about me

i show up, and things go right.

I'm a recent MBA grad from UC Riverside's Anderson School of Management who fell into event marketing and never left. Over the past several years, I've represented brands like Canva, YouTube, Marvel, and Teen Vogue at national activations, and led a team of 32 ambassadors across 115+ events as Graduate Ambassador and Event Director.

That experience taught me how brands build real trust and connection with the people who show up for them. I'm especially drawn to beauty, fashion, and experiential marketing, and I'm always looking for the next event, brand, or community worth building.
